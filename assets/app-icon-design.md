# Rose Nocturne

Rose Nocturne treats a tiny application icon as a carefully lit miniature portrait rather than a compressed illustration. The character's recognisable pink silhouette leads; generous negative space and a single calm frame let the portrait survive the Dock's smallest sizes. The composition should feel meticulously crafted and instantly distinct without relying on letters or labels.

A deep plum ground gives the pastel subject definition, while controlled rose light warms the face and moves attention inward. Color has a structural role: a soft halo separates the original transparent character art from its surroundings, and a restrained rim gives the tile the weight of a real object. Every tonal transition must feel painstakingly calibrated, never like an indiscriminate filter.

Scale is the instrument of clarity. The original full-body drawing is used only as source material; the icon crops to head and shoulders so the eyes and pink hair become its visual signature. Sparse concentric accents suggest gentle motion behind the figure, but no small marks compete with the face. The hierarchy should read at sixteen pixels and reward close inspection at full size.

The silhouette may quietly evoke the intimacy of a character staying beside someone at their desk. Rounded corners and transparent outer margins belong to the macOS visual system, not to a square screenshot. No typography is needed: the finest execution comes from master-level attention to contour, spacing, antialiasing, and a polished integration of the approved original drawing.
